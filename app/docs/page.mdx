# Welcome to QueueLens

## Background job monitoring made simple.

QueueLens is a developer-focused observability platform for monitoring Celery background tasks.

It helps you understand what happens to your background jobs without manually instrumenting every task or building your own monitoring infrastructure.

With QueueLens, you can:

- Monitor Celery task execution.
- Track task lifecycle events.
- Identify failed tasks and understand why they failed.
- Inspect exception types, error messages, and tracebacks.
- Monitor retries and execution status.
- View your background job activity through a centralized dashboard.

## How it works

QueueLens integrates directly with your existing Celery application through a lightweight Python SDK.

The SDK listens to Celery lifecycle signals, collects relevant execution information, and securely sends events to the QueueLens platform.

Your existing Celery application, broker, and workers remain under your control. QueueLens does not execute or manage your background jobs.

## Get started

1. Create your QueueLens account.
2. Sign in using Google.
3. Create an API key from your dashboard.
4. Install the QueueLens Python SDK.
5. Configure your API key.
6. Instrument your Celery application.
7. Start monitoring your jobs.

**Your existing Celery workflow stays the same. QueueLens adds observability on top of it.**
